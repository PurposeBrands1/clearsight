ClearSight Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Overview

ClearSight ("we", "our", "the app") is a magnifying glass and text reading app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • Camera: The app accesses your device camera solely to provide real-time magnification. Camera images are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted, stored, or shared.
  • Text Recognition: OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is performed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Recognized text is not stored or transmitted anywhere except when you choose to use the "Read Aloud" feature.
  • Text-to-Speech: When you tap "Read Aloud", the recognized text is sent to OpenAI's text-to-speech API to generate natural-sounding audio. This text is processed by OpenAI according to their privacy policy. No other data is sent.
  • Settings: Your preferences (zoom level, language, contrast mode, speech rate) are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. They are not transmitted anywhere.

Camera Usage

ClearSight requires camera access to function as a magnifying glass. The camera feed is displayed in real-time on your screen and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted. You can revoke camera access at any time in your device's Settings.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service:

  • OpenAI API — Used solely for text-to-speech conversion when you tap "Read Aloud". Only the recognized text is sent. No personal information, images, or device data is transmitted. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

All app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not use cloud storage, accounts, or login systems. We do not have access to any data on your device.

Analytics & Tracking

ClearSight does not include any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage or behavior in any way.

Children's Privacy

ClearSight does not knowingly collect any data from children. The app does not collect data from any user regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
